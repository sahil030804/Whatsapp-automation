# NovaTech Solutions — Company Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

NovaTech Solutions is a B2B SaaS company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2018 by Elena Vasquez and David Chen, the company provides AI-powered customer engagement tools for mid-market businesses. As of 2025, NovaTech has 340 employees across offices in Austin, Dublin, and Singapore. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) is $47M.

**Mission:** Help every business deliver personalized customer experiences at scale.

## Billing & Refund Policy

All plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual billing receives a 20% discount. Refunds are available for annual plans only within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are non-refundable but can be cancelled anytime. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. Enterprise contracts are subject to terms defined in the Master Services Agreement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I switch plans mid-cycle?**
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Do you offer a free trial?**
A: Yes, a 14-day free trial on Starter and Pro plans. No credit card required.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my API call limit?**
A: You will receive a warning at 80% usage. Additional calls are blocked once the limit is reached. You can purchase overage packs or upgrade your plan.

**Q: Is my data used to train AI models?**
A: No. Customer data is never used for model training. Each tenant operates in an isolated environment.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes. You can export all conversations, analytics, and configuration via the dashboard or API at any time.
